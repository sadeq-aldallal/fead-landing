# Spec Requirements Document

> Spec: Legal Compliance System
> Created: 2025-08-28

## Overview

Implement a comprehensive legal compliance system to meet Meta/Instagram app approval requirements and international data protection regulations for fead.app's SaaS customer support AI platform. This system will ensure GDPR compliance, Meta developer policy adherence, and automated data deletion capabilities required for business app approval.

## User Stories

### Business Owner Data Protection

As a business owner using fead.app's AI customer support, I want to know that my customer data is protected according to international privacy laws, so that I can use the platform confidently without worrying about legal compliance issues.

The system will automatically handle data protection requirements, provide clear privacy disclosures, and ensure all customer interactions through Instagram are compliant with Meta's terms of service. Business owners will have visibility into data handling practices through clear documentation.

### Platform Admin Meta Compliance

As a fead.app platform administrator, I want to ensure our app meets all Meta/Instagram Business API requirements, so that we can successfully get approved as a tech provider and maintain our Instagram integration.

The compliance system will implement automated data deletion workflows, proper AI agent disclosures, and maintain all required legal documents (privacy policy, terms of service) that comply with both Dutch law and international requirements.

### End Customer Data Rights

As an end customer interacting with AI agents on Instagram, I want clear disclosure when I'm talking to an AI agent and the ability to request human support or data deletion, so that I understand my rights and can control my personal data.

The system will automatically include AI disclosure in responses, provide 24-hour windows for escalation to human agents, and maintain transparent data deletion processes without account restoration options.

## Spec Scope

1. **GDPR-Compliant Privacy Policy** - Generate comprehensive privacy policy covering Netherlands company operations, international data transfers, and Instagram data processing
2. **Meta-Compliant Terms of Service** - Create terms that align with Meta developer policies, include AI agent disclosures, and cover SaaS subscription terms
3. **Automated Data Deletion System** - Enhance existing deletion workflows to meet Meta requirements with proper audit trails and no restoration capabilities
4. **Cookie Policy and Data Processing Agreements** - Standard web compliance documents for enterprise customers and website visitors
5. **AI Agent Disclosure Framework** - Implement required disclosures for automated customer support interactions and human escalation processes

## Out of Scope

- Admin dashboard for compliance monitoring (not requested)
- Manual data restoration capabilities (specifically excluded to maintain transparency)
- Real-time consent tracking interfaces (beyond basic requirement)
- Advanced compliance reporting features
- Integration with third-party legal review services

## Expected Deliverable

1. All legal documents automatically generated and displayed correctly on fead.app website with proper links and accessibility
2. Meta app approval submission ready with all required compliance documentation and automated data handling processes
3. International law compliance verified for Netherlands-based company serving global customers through Instagram Business API integration

This implementation will ensure fead.app meets all requirements for Meta business app approval while maintaining GDPR compliance and supporting the platform's international customer base.
