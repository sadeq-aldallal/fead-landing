# Product Mission (Lite)

fead.app is a SaaS customer support AI agent configuration platform that helps Instagram business owners automate customer interactions by providing intelligent, multilingual AI agents tailored for service and retail business types.

**Current Phase:** MVP - SaaS platform serving Instagram-based service providers and retail businesses who need automated, multilingual customer support. Unlike generic chatbot solutions, fead.app provides Instagram-native integration with business type-specific AI workflows that handle appointments for service businesses and orders for retail businesses, supporting both English and Arabic languages with multi-tenant SaaS architecture.
